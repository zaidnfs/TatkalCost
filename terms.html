2. Compliance with the Railways Act, 1989 (Section 143)

Section 143 of the Railways Act, 1989 prohibits unauthorized businesses of procuring, transferring, or selling railway tickets. TatkalCost explicitly states that:

  • This platform does not sell, resell, hold, or procure railway tickets.
  • This platform does not automate railway booking forms, CAPTCHA fields, or passenger registration.
  • No booking fees or ticket commission charges are levied.
  • All ticket purchases must be executed directly by the individual passenger on official railway booking channels (irctc.co.in or UTS mobile apps).
