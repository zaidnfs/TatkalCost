IRCTC eWallet Architectural & Payment Rules

The IRCTC eWallet is a closed-system prepaid payment instrument (PPI) created to eliminate the banking redirection and OTP wait, enabling 1-click checkout during the critical 120-second Tatkal rush.

  • One-time Registration: ₹50 + 18% GST (₹59.00), non-refundable. Requires Aadhaar and PAN verification.
  • Per-Booking Charge: ₹10 + 18% GST (₹11.80) per transaction regardless of passenger count.
  • Deposit Constraints: Minimum top-up is ₹100; maximum balance allowed is ₹10,000.
  • Zero-Fee Top-Up: Always deposit money into eWallet via UPI. Using Net Banking or Credit Cards to load the wallet may trigger payment gateway surcharges.
  • Balance Recovery: Balance cannot be withdrawn as cash directly. However, under official Ministry directives, closing your eWallet account transfers unspent funds back to your linked bank account.

Tatkal Booking Protocols & Quota Timings

Tatkal reservations open exactly one day in advance of train journey date from the train originating station:

  • 10:00 AM IST: AC Classes open (1A has no Tatkal; covers 2A, 3A, 3E, CC, EC).
  • 11:00 AM IST: Non-AC Classes open (Sleeper and Second Sitting 2S).
  • 30-Minute Agent Restriction: Authorised railway travel agents are barred from booking during the first 30 minutes (10:00 to 10:30 AM and 11:00 to 11:30 AM) to prioritize direct passengers.
  • Max 4 Passengers: Unlike general reservations which permit 6 passengers, a Tatkal ticket allows a maximum of 4 passengers per PNR.
  • Compulsory Aadhaar OTP: User profiles must be Aadhaar-verified on IRCTC to book during the peak Tatkal opening window.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clarifications on convenience fees, payment failures, and refund rules.

The most common cause is having insufficient funds by just a few rupees. Many passengers top up only the ticket fare (e.g., ₹1,950), forgetting that IRCTC adds ₹35.40 in convenience fees and ₹11.80 for the eWallet transaction. At the final screen, the total is ₹1,997.20. IRCTC does not allow mid-session wallet top-ups, causing instant transaction failure and seat loss.
The IRCTC convenience fee is charged strictly per transaction (per PNR), not per passenger. Whether you book for 1 passenger or 4 passengers on the same ticket, you pay the convenience fee only once (₹23.60 via UPI or ₹35.40 via Non-UPI/eWallet for AC classes).
As a standard rule, no refund is granted on the cancellation of confirmed Tatkal tickets. Full fare refunds (less clerkage) are only issued under special operational circumstances, such as: train cancelled by railways, train running more than 3 hours late at journey originating station, or train diverted and the passenger chooses not to travel.
No, the eWallet is actually more expensive per booking. While UPI attracts a convenience fee of ₹11.80 (Non-AC) or ₹23.60 (AC) with ₹0 gateway fee, eWallet attracts the higher Non-UPI convenience fee (₹17.70 or ₹35.40) PLUS a ₹11.80 eWallet fee. Passengers use eWallet purely for speed and reliability, avoiding bank OTP delays during the intense 2-minute Tatkal window.
